The whole rule, in one breath

Drop a lamp on an open square and light pours down its row and column until a wall stops it. Light every square, never let two lamps see each other, and give each numbered wall exactly the lamps it asks for. One fair answer, every time — no guessing.

The part you feel

A lamp lands with a crisp tap. Light spilling across the grid has its own soft ripple. A clue clicking shut feels nothing like two lamps flaring into conflict — a sharp buzz you'll sense before you even look. Solve the board and it blooms under your fingertips.

Today's board, and a friend's

One fresh puzzle a day, the same for everyone, gentle on Monday and tight by the weekend. When a board feels good, fold it into a QR code and a link — a friend scans it and plays the exact same puzzle. No account, no server, nothing between you and the challenge.

Free to play, yours to keep

The daily and the first two packs are free for good. One optional unlock — $3.99, paid once, never a subscription — opens every pack, the daily archive, an endless practice mode, and extra haptic textures and themes. No ads ever interrupt you, and the whole game runs on a plane with no signal.
