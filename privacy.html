Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Glint is built to be private by default. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. Glint has no account, no login, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. Your puzzle progress, streaks, and settings are stored only on your device.

Permissions

Glint requests no permissions. It does not access your camera, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Glint Pro" purchase is processed by Apple and validated through RevenueCat. This involves only a non-personal purchase receipt; we never receive your name, email, or payment details.

Challenge links

The "challenge a friend" feature encodes a puzzle into a link or QR code. The puzzle is contained entirely within the link — no data is sent to or stored on any server.

Children

Glint is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.
